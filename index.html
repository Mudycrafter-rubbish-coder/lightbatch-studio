Practical details

What it does.
And what it doesn't.

Is it a Lightroom replacement?

No. It is a focused batch-matching utility, not a catalog, mask editor, RAW demosaicer, or layered cloud service.

Can it fix mixed lighting?

It works best when the photos share broadly similar light. Mixed foreground/background color may still need individual work.

Does it preserve originals?

Yes. The app writes new files and never edits the source photos.

What formats can it open?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and TIFF. HEIC and camera RAW are not supported in version 1.0.

Why is Windows showing an unknown publisher?

The initial release is not code-signed. Verify the published SHA-256 checksum before running it.
