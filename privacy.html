Privacy

Photos stay local.

Image processing. LightBatch does not upload photos, filenames, folder names, analytics, or usage telemetry.

Metadata. Camera and location metadata is removed by default. Users can explicitly choose to retain compatible metadata.

License checks. The app sends only a public product identifier and a license key to the payment provider. A short offline grace period applies.

Payments. Card entry and subscription billing happen on the payment provider's hosted checkout, never inside the desktop application.

Local files. License data is encrypted with the current Windows account. Batch manifests and exported images stay in the chosen output folder.
